Project: Berlin vs Munich: a short-term rental market analysis for a property manager

Business problem: A property-management company wants to start managing short-term rentals in Berlin and Munich. Today it decides where to list and how to price based on guesswork.

Stakeholders (fictional scenario):

Owner: decides where to invest, needs a clear recommendation
Operations and pricing manager: needs price benchmarks by area and listing type

Business questions:

Which neighbourhoods have the highest estimated revenue per listing?
How do Berlin and Munich differ in price, size, ratings and room types?
How do professional hosts differ from individual hosts?
What drives the nightly price?
When is guest activity highest?
Recommendation: which areas and listing types should the company focus on?

KPIs (definitions to finalise in v2): active listings, median nightly price, estimated occupancy, estimated revenue per listing, share of professional hosts, average rating.

Data: Inside Airbnb, Berlin and Munich, scrape dates: Berlin data scraped on 19-09-2026, Munich data scraped on 23-09-2026.

Assumptions (v1): revenue is estimated, not real; a “professional host” is a host with more than one listing (threshold may change); prices are in the local currency.

Limitations: one snapshot per city; availability is not the same as bookings.

Out of scope: the calendar file, a price-prediction model.

Deliverables: SQL scripts and ERD, Tableau dashboard, README, 5-minute presentation, Power BI report (after the presentation).
